SnapAd Network

SnapAd Network is a platform concept that brings professional photography and startup advertising together. The website introduces services for people and teams looking to capture events, promote a brand, or get personalized help.

**Live site:** [studio-ochre-one.vercel.app](https://studio-ochre-one.vercel.app/)

## What it offers

- **Professional photography** — Book photographers for weddings, corporate events, and personal shoots.
- **Startup advertising** — Promote a brand through online and offline advertising channels.
- **Personalized support** — The site describes support through scheduled Zoom meetings.
- **Service recommendations** — The site describes recommendations tailored to a visitor's history.

## Website

The live website includes an introductory landing page, service highlights, and customer testimonials. The service descriptions above reflect what the site presents; they do not imply that booking, advertising, Zoom scheduling, or recommendation features are fully implemented in this repository.
